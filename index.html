System Design

LESSON 06
Consistency & Replication: A Like-Counter Under Concurrent Writes
A viral link takes 50,000 likes/sec and the count goes up by one, not two — the lost update. Atomic increment to fix the write, replication to scale reads, replication lag and read-your-writes, strong vs eventual, quorums (W+R>N), and the sharded counter.
LESSON 05
Async Work: Queues, Workers, and the 2-Million-Link Job
A bulk job that would run 2.8 hours inline — pushed onto a queue and drained by workers in ~8 minutes. Visibility timeouts, at-least-once vs idempotency, poison messages + dead-letter queues, ordering by partition, and backpressure.
LESSON 04
Consistent Hashing: Add a Shard Without the Storm
Adding a 17th shard moved 94% of keys — here's the circle that drops it to ~6%. The hash ring, clockwise ownership, and virtual nodes for even load and graceful failure.
LESSON 03
Scaling Writes: One Counter, Many Shards
When 40,000 writes/sec break the single ID counter and the single disk: distributed ID generation (ID blocks vs Snowflake), hash vs range sharding, and why adding a shard reshuffles 94% of keys.
LESSON 02
Scaling Reads: Eviction, CDNs, and the Stampede
Why a 1% cache serves 96% of reads, LRU vs TTL eviction, the cache stampede (thundering herd) and how to stop it, then CDNs and the speed of light.
LESSON 01
Design a URL Shortener
Estimation, data model, counter+Base62 codes, read/write paths, 301 vs 302, and the first bottleneck → caching.
